05 — AI journey

A pair for the new stack. Not a substitute for it.

I am using AI to learn Electron, Angular, and TypeScript faster. The rule is simple: it may draft and explain. I decide what stays, and I have to be able to read it.

Nine years of Qt did not teach me Angular’s compiler. An assistant is good at that gap. It can name the decorator, show the route shape, and point at the page in the docs. It is bad at knowing which part of a private product I am allowed to describe, and it will sound sure when it is wrong.

So the practice is narrow. I ask it to explain an error before I ask it to patch one. I keep experiments small enough to finish. I write the notes myself. When the model and the compiler disagree, I trust the compiler and the official docs.

What I am willing to publish

Learning notes, this website, and small public studies. Product source, customer data, and anything that belongs to the company stay off the page and out of the chat.
